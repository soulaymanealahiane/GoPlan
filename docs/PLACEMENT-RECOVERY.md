# BBA semester placement recovery

The November 2025 BBA program sheet, page 2, explicitly requires MTH1305,
Mathematics for Business, for 3 credits. The catalogue importer previously left
this course as a provisional option. Automatic selection excluded it, leaving
the mathematics requirement without a course code. Accounting, Economics and
Business Statistics then had no scheduled mathematics prerequisite, blocking
their dependent business courses as well.

The synthetic pre-fix Analytics and AI for Business / FinTech fixture reproduces
21 unplaced courses totaling 60 credits. The correction resolves the confirmed
single-course mathematics requirement before model preferences are applied.
Language, Arabic, civic and school-approval requirements retain their personal
confirmation rules. Second-concentration options use that concentration's
prerequisite rules when checking eligibility.

Older generated BBA plans with this exact missing-mathematics cascade open a
corrected preview; the saved original remains until the student saves the
preview. Recorded study, notes, choices and account allowances are retained.
Deliberately edited personal plans and recorded or overridden mathematics are
not automatically regenerated. Old semester receipts cannot restore the broken
sequence. Degree checks separately disclose any courses awaiting placement.

`node verify-business-placement.mjs` covers the pre-fix fixture, 324 BBA
concentration/load/summer combinations, the current hybrid agents, stale receipt
rejection, recovery idempotence, preserved study/notes, manual-edit isolation and
authenticated save/reload with no AI changes remaining. Providers and accounts
are synthetic; no live inference or email delivery is required.
