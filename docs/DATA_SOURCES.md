# Academic data provenance

GoPlan's reviewed dataset is derived from AUI program sheets, course sequences, the academic catalogue and exchange documentation supplied during development. Curated opportunity records also cite institutional and company sources. Dates and source references are retained in the data modules.

The application is independent of AUI. Source documents may have their own copyright and usage restrictions. Full PDF extracts (`dist/source-pages.json`), original files and personal degree-plan documents are excluded from the public export. Public code includes the curated planning model needed to inspect the implementation; publication is not a claim that third-party materials are openly licensed.

Do not add student names, grades, transcripts or identifiable plans to fixtures. Use synthetic inputs. Revalidate academic rules when the applicable catalogue changes; a catalogue listing is not proof that a course is offered in a particular term.

The reviewed opportunity pool contains 40 Moroccan internship targets and 38 institutions already in the supplied AUI exchange list. `data/guidance/opportunities-variety-v13.json` records the additional official sources and restrictions reviewed on 19 October 2026. Academic fit is an inference; the source term, exchange scheme and existing limitations remain authoritative.
