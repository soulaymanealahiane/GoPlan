# Morocco employer pool — November 2026

The pool contains **185 employer targets**, up from 92: 93 additions, supported by 159 distinct primary-source URLs. This includes Moroccan firms and multinational employers with documented Moroccan operations. Targets are operating organizations; plants, hotel brands, renamed companies and business units are not counted as independent additions. Some existing targets are public utilities or policy organizations. Separate operating ventures can share an investor.

Academic fit is inferred from documented activities, not a verified vacancy, internship agreement, eligibility guarantee or selection probability. Recruitment availability must be confirmed with the employer. Source-specific ownership and evidence limitations are retained in each record. Hotel employing entities can differ by property.
