## Agreed startup direction

Universities fund pilots for approved student cohorts. Prioritize students admitted and matriculated before their first fall semester; continuing students follow the same workflow and include prior study at the final review. Each university edition requires reviewed program data and its own deployment/database. The current prepared academic edition is the independent AUI prototype. No university endorsement, paid customer, retention or impact claim has been established.

Read UNIVERSITY_VISION.md for roster imports, authentication boundaries and pilot evaluation. Existing operation counters are events, not unique students or retention metrics.
